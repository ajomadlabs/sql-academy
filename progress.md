# Progress Tracker

Fill this in daily. It takes two minutes and it's the difference between a plan and an intention.

---

## Why track

Confidence is a lagging indicator — it arrives weeks after the competence does. In the meantime, this file is the evidence that progress is real. On the days it doesn't feel like it's working, the tracker is the thing that says otherwise.

## Weekly review

Every Sunday, ten minutes:

1. Which checkpoints were hit?
2. What's still shaky and needs re-drilling?
3. What did the error log reveal this week?
4. Applications sent vs target?
5. What changes next week?

## When it stops feeling worth it

It will, somewhere around week 5. That's the normal shape of this: the early progress is fast and visible, the middle is slow, and rejections start arriving at the same time.

Two things to reach for on those days:

**Read back through the first week of this log.** The gap between Day 5 and Day 35 is large and completely invisible from the inside.

**Cut the day short rather than skipping it.** Two focused hours on a bad day preserves the habit. A skipped day becomes a skipped week — the streak is doing more work here than any individual session.
