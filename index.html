Every day tells you exactly what to do: one goal, the concepts explained with worked examples, the mistakes to expect, and problems you solve against a database with real mess in it — nullable emails, unshipped orders, duplicate lines, gaps in the calendar.

Free. No card, now or later.

How it works

  1. Open the day you are on. You are never deciding what to study next. Forty-eight days, ordered so nothing depends on something you have not met.
  2. Read the day, then solve it. The lesson is written out properly — why it works, not just the syntax — and then you write queries.
  3. Answers get checked, not self-reported. Your query runs against a real database and the result is compared. Written your way, with your own aliases, it still passes. When it fails you are told how the result differed — never the answer.

What this is, and what it is not

  • It is free. There is no paid tier, no trial, and no card — not at the start and not later.
  • You can see everything before signing in. The full curriculum is further down this page. You do not sign in to find out what you are getting.
  • Signing in is for your progress, not for us. It is how the course follows you from laptop to phone. We store the email and name Google gives us, and what you have finished. That is the whole list.
  • No ads, and no email. Signing in does not put you on a list. There is no sequence, no newsletter, and nothing to unsubscribe from.
  • No certificate. Nobody is impressed by one. Being able to write the query in the interview is the thing that counts.
  • Nothing expires and nothing is lost. Stop for three months and it is exactly where you left it.

Before you start

What you need

A browser. Nothing to install, no database to set up, no account with anyone except the Google sign-in. It assumes you have never written SQL.

Who it is for

People who use SQL occasionally and would like to stop guessing, and people preparing for interviews who can follow a tutorial but freeze on a window function under pressure.
